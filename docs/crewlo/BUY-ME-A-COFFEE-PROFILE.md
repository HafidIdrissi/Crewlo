Hi, I'm Hafid Idrissi, the developer behind Crewlo. I'm building a visual studio
where coding agents have their own place to work. You can send a mission, follow
their activity, and find their replies in one workspace.

Crewlo is an early preview. I share its progress, design decisions, and verified
examples through the project and its blog.

helps me spend more time improving the studio, testing installers, and
